Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your financial data is personal, and keeping it safe is central to Spendly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type, to help keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you enter to provide and personalise the service: showing your expenses, summaries, and spending insights back to you. Your email address is used to manage your account and, only if you opt in, to send you important service updates. We do not sell your personal data or use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers and protected with appropriate technical measures. We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data in accordance with our retention and deletion procedures.

4. Third Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services for essential infrastructure such as hosting and analytics. These providers are permitted to process your data only to the extent necessary to perform their functions and are bound by confidentiality obligations. We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete the expense data you have entered at any time through your account. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights over your personal data, which we will honour where applicable.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us through the account support channels, and we will respond as promptly as we can.
